SehatAM privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

SehatAM is an app for logging what you eat and tracking your weight. This page explains what the app does with your information.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter (food items, weights, goals and settings) is stored only on the phone or in the browser where you use the app. The app has no accounts and no server. We do not receive, see or store any of it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect personal information, and it contains no advertising or tracking.

Backups

"Export my data" creates a file containing your entries and settings. The file goes only where you choose to save or send it. Anyone who has the file can read it, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Reminders

If you switch reminders on, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; no information leaves the device.

Deleting your data

"Delete all data" in Settings permanently removes everything the app has stored. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in a browser, does the same.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Later versions of the app may add optional features, such as sign-in, cloud backup or calorie estimates, that send data off your device. This policy will be updated before any such feature is released, and those features will be off unless you choose to use them.
